# Canopy composition brief

Create a 1920×1080 dark-first product film from real captures of the deployed Canopy app. Use the existing graphite, lime, and lavender palette. The composition should feel like a financial terminal crossed with a premium collectible reveal.

The hook and close may use full-frame type. All middle beats must show the actual interface inside a subtle browser frame. Animate only transform and opacity. Use slow 1.02–1.08 camera pushes, short 10–16-frame text entrances, and 8-frame cuts/crossfades. Keep captions in a consistent lower-left safe area and preserve enough hold time to read them muted.

The stock catalog is the center of the story. Make xStocks and PreStocks names legible. The instant screen must visibly retain the selected token. The vault and DNA scenes should communicate that financial inputs determine the collectible. Finish with three concise proof points and the live URL.

Audio is an original low-volume synthetic pulse with small transition clicks. It must never compete with the text.
